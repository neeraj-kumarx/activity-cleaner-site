Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Insta Bulk Unliker.

What does Insta Bulk Unliker do?

Insta Bulk Unliker helps you unlike liked posts from the supported activity page in controlled batches.

How does it work?

You choose a batch size in the extension and press Start. The extension operates on the supported activity page in your browser.

Can I stop the process?

Yes. The extension has a Stop button that allows you to halt a running cleanup.

Does Insta Bulk Unliker store my Instagram password?

No. The extension does not collect or store your Instagram login credentials.

Does Insta Bulk Unliker send my activity to a server?

No developer-controlled server is used by the extension for processing. The cleanup is performed locally in your browser.

Is Insta Bulk Unliker affiliated with Instagram?

No. Insta Bulk Unliker is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta.

What happens if Instagram changes its website?

Changes to the supported activity page may affect how the extension works. The extension may need an update when the website changes.
