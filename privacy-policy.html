Privacy Policy

Insta Activity Cleaner does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

Data Handling

The extension works locally inside your browser. It does not send Instagram data, passwords, messages, browsing history, or account information to any external server.

Permissions

The extension uses browser permissions only to perform user-requested cleanup actions such as removing likes, saved posts, and reposts on Instagram activity pages.

Third Party Services

This extension is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
