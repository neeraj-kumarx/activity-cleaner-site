Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Overview

Insta Activity Cleaner processes page elements locally in your browser only to perform cleanup actions that you explicitly start.

Data Handling

Insta Activity Cleaner does not:

  • Collect, sell, transfer, or store personal information on external servers.
  • Collect login credentials or passwords.
  • Collect messages, post content, or profile data.
  • Collect browsing history.
  • Collect payment information.

The extension stores your selected batch size and theme preference locally using Chrome extension storage.

Insta Activity Cleaner does not send your activity to a developer-controlled server.

Permissions

Active Tab: Used to interact with the supported Instagram page when you start Insta Activity Cleaner and, when necessary, navigate to the relevant Instagram activity page (Likes, Saved, or Reposts) for the mode you selected.

Storage: Used to remember local extension settings such as your batch size and theme preference.

Instagram Site Access: Used so Insta Activity Cleaner can operate on supported Instagram activity pages after you start a cleanup.

User Control

Cleanup actions begin only after you start the process from the extension. You can stop a running cleanup at any time.

You may also disable or remove Insta Activity Cleaner from Chrome at any time.

Third-Party Services

Insta Activity Cleaner operates on Instagram pages but is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta.

Your use of Instagram remains subject to Instagram's own terms and policies.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Insta Activity Cleaner's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
